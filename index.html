Why GreenCurrent?

Flooding can become more damaging when waterways are blocked by litter and communities lack resilient infrastructure. Our concept combines cleanup work with climate awareness.

🌊 Cleaner waterways

Support community cleanup efforts that keep litter out of streams and rivers.

🏘️ Resilient communities

Highlight projects that improve awareness and preparedness around flooding.

📚 Climate education

Make climate information easier for students and families to understand.
